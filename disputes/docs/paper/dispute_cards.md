# Paper dispute cards (SPECIMEN)

Print one set per table. Fill queue (FRAUD or GENERAL), reviewer (SENIOR or STANDARD) and readiness (READY, NEEDS_INFO or LOOKUP_UNAVAILABLE) by hand, with the reason for each.

| --- | --- | --- |

| B | "I didn't make this purchase." 1 charge of 1,450.00. | The charge is on the card. |
