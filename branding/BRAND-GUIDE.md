# Puffin & Pine: internal identity

**Fictional training brand.** Puffin & Pine is a business bank with a grounded, capable and human character. The puffin suggests attentiveness and navigation; the pine suggests stability and long-term growth. This identity is for the lab, not a real financial institution.

## Brand architecture

- **Bank:** Puffin & Pine. Use the full name in introductions; “P&P” is an informal internal abbreviation.
- **Internal tool family:** **Pineway**. Use “Pineway | Onboarding” for this workspace and reserve the same pattern for future tools, such as “Pineway | Case Review.”
- **Internal promise:** **Clear paths. Sound decisions.** A supporting line for onboarding and training materials, not a claim about automated approval.
- **Working principle:** “Make the next step clear.” Use in internal communications, training and empty states; avoid treating it as a compliance guarantee.

## Logo files

- `puffin-pine-concept.png`: the preferred puffin-and-pine emblem, used in the dashboard.
- `mark.svg`, `logo-dark.svg`, and `logo-light.svg`: earlier alternate vector explorations; these are not the preferred logo.

Preserve the emblem's proportions and allow space around it. Avoid stretching, recoloring individual parts, crowding the mark or placing it on a busy photograph. Place the bank name as separate readable text when needed.

## Color and type

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep pine | `#102E2E` | Navigation and dark surfaces |
| Evergreen | `#17483F` | Primary brand and emblem |
| Sage | `#B5D5A8` | Emblem and quiet highlights |
| Mist | `#F5F7F6` | Workspace background |
| Ink | `#1B2E32` | Primary text |
| Amber | `#D8A66C` | Small identity accent, not a status indicator |

Use Manrope for product headings and DM Sans for interface text when available, with system sans-serif fallbacks. The wordmark uses a serif face for a bank-like editorial character. Keep body copy legible and never convey a status through color alone.

## Internal-product conventions

- Use a consistent product name, bank lockup and navigation. Label the environment visibly: **DEMO**, **TEST** or **PRODUCTION**, as applicable.
- Identify the owner and support route on real deployments; in this lab use “Onboarding team · Demo workspace.”
- Use plain-language status labels, timestamps and named next actions. Distinguish “ready for analyst” from an approval.
- Label simulated data, simulated integrations and model-generated content. Make human ownership and outstanding work visible.
- Use a restrained, helpful tone: “Review the missing evidence” rather than “Error!”; do not imply a check passed when it has not run.
- Reuse the mark and color tokens across onboarding, training slides, documentation and internal applications. Add a tool descriptor rather than inventing a new bank logo for every team.

## Example internal copy

**Welcome:** “Pineway brings the details together so you can move each case forward with confidence.”

**Empty queue:** “You're all caught up. New applications will appear here.”

**Unavailable service:** “Screening is unavailable. The case is on hold until a result is recorded.”

**Human review:** “A person must review this result before the case can move forward.”

**Training footer:** “Puffin & Pine is fictional. This workspace uses synthetic data.”
